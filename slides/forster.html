Which regime?

|V| ≪ λ (reorganisation, disorder): incoherent Förster hops. |V| ≳ λ: delocalised excitons (Redfield). PSI’s ~1 nm Chl spacing sits in between: coupled clusters hopping to each other.

[8:50–9:50] When coupling is weak compared with disorder and reorganisation, energy hops incoherently: Förster theory. The rate goes as κ squared, the donor's radiative rate and the spectral overlap J, over R to the sixth. The efficiency, k_ET over the sum of all rates, becomes 1 over 1 plus (R over R0) to the sixth. Neighbouring pigments sit about 1 nm apart, far inside R0, so every hop is essentially lossless. When coupling dominates you get delocalised excitons that oscillate, the teal curve; real antennas sit in between, with coupled clusters hopping to each other. The bottom line is rate competition: trapping in about 25 ps against loss in 6 ns gives a yield of 99.6 percent, the number an artificial system has to match. But Förster is not the only mechanism.
