[22:53–23:44] Does any of this give a real material? The strongest case runs the whole loop. A 2026 JACS study used machine-learning interatomic potentials to predict which dopants stabilise orthorhombic Sn3O4: boron, aluminium, strontium and yttrium. Hydrothermal synthesis and X-ray diffraction confirmed the phase, and aluminium gave the best visible-light hydrogen production. The institute's release quotes about 16 times the undoped rate, so treat that as a press figure; 5 percent aluminium was optimal in films. The older landmark is Burger's mobile robotic chemist: 688 experiments in 8 days over 10 variables, using Bayesian optimisation to pick each experiment, finding a formulation about six times more active.
