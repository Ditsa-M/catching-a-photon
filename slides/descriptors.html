[20:30–21:30] A fast filter is only as good as what it is asked to look for. Hu and colleagues, Nature Catalysis 2025, built descriptors for the three coupled steps of a molecular CO2-reduction system: photosensitisation, electron transfer and catalysis. That is the same chain as the reaction centre; the dashed lines map each biological step onto the artificial one. They screened 3,444 photosensitiser–catalyst systems and about 180,000 conformations, predicted six candidates, and the best reached a turnover number of 4,390, with time-resolved spectroscopy confirming the descriptors. The maths is in the bars: system efficiency is a product, so 0.9 × 0.9 × 0.2 is only 0.16. The weakest step dominates, in a leaf or a flask.
