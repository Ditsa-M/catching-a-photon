[25:04–25:43] The literature also shows how far the design can go. Chlorophyll f drives PSI charge separation at 745 nm, and far-red PSI uses light to about 800 nm with only four chlorophyll f sites. On the acceptor side, tethering FB directly to a hydrogenase gives 105 electrons per PSI per second, twice the in-vivo rate, because nothing has to diffuse. That's today's lesson as a device: keep the trap open by clearing electrons fast. The reviews cited here map out the rest of the design space.
